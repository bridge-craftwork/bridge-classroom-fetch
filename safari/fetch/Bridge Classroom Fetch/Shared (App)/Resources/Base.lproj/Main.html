Getting started

  1. Turn the extension on in Safari’s settings, under Extensions.
  2. Open your results: ACBL Live, ACBL Live for Clubs or Bridge Base Online.
  3. Click the extension’s button in Safari’s toolbar, choose Always Allow on Every Website, then reload the page. It still runs only on the results sites and Bridge Classroom.
  4. Analyze in Bridge Classroom appears on your results pages.

Until Safari allows it, the extension shows nothing, and its toolbar button is grey. You can also allow the sites in Safari’s settings: Extensions, then Edit Websites…

Getting started

  1. In the Settings app, open Apps, Safari, Extensions, then Bridge Classroom Fetch, and turn on Allow Extension.
  2. In Safari, open your results: ACBL Live, ACBL Live for Clubs or Bridge Base Online.
  3. Tap the page menu at the left end of the address bar (on iPhone the address bar is usually at the bottom), tap Bridge Classroom Fetch, choose Always Allow on Every Website, then reload the page. It still runs only on the results sites and Bridge Classroom.
  4. Analyze in Bridge Classroom appears on your results pages.

Until Safari allows it, the extension shows nothing.
